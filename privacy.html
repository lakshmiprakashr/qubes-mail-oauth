Privacy Policy

How Qubes Mail accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you connect it to Gmail.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Qubes Mail application (the "app") and the project website at https://lakshmiprakashr.github.io/qubes-mail-oauth/ (the "site"). It explains how the app handles Google user data when you authorize it to access a Gmail account.

This policy does not apply to Gmail or other Google services. Those services are governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Qubes Mail is a private, local-first email client for Qubes OS. It is not a public email service and is not affiliated with Google LLC.

Summary

Qubes Mail connects directly to Gmail, stores your mail and OAuth tokens on your own computer, and does not send your email to a Qubes Mail-operated server. Optional AI processing, if enabled, runs on equipment you control. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Information accessed from Google

When you authorize Qubes Mail with your Google Account, Google provides the app with:

  • the email address associated with the account;
  • OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens;
  • the Gmail data permitted by the scopes you approve.

The app requests Google's full Gmail access scope (https://mail.google.com/), which is required for IMAP and SMTP access using OAuth 2.0. Google describes this scope as permission to read, compose, send, and permanently delete email in your Gmail account.

Qubes Mail uses this access to:

  • download messages and attachments from the folders you choose to sync, which are Inbox, Sent, Drafts, and Trash by default;
  • display, search, and organize those local copies;
  • send messages you compose through Gmail's SMTP service.

Qubes Mail does not request access to Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google services.

How information is used

Qubes Mail uses Google user data only to provide and improve its user-facing email features on your device:

  • authenticate to Gmail and refresh access tokens;
  • synchronize the folders you select;
  • display, search, and organize your local mail;
  • send messages you explicitly compose.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, advertising-related profiling, or eligibility decisions such as creditworthiness or lending. We do not sell it.

If optional mail-analysis or AI features are enabled, the message content needed for the requested analysis is processed by a local model service on equipment you control. Qubes Mail does not send Google user data to an external AI service. The developer does not receive that content.

Local storage and retention

Synced mail, attachments, the local search index, and application settings are stored on your Qubes OS computer. OAuth access and refresh tokens are also stored there, encrypted at rest. Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed by the app as needed.

Qubes Mail does not operate a server that stores your mail or tokens. The developer does not receive a copy of your email through the app.

Data remains on your device until you delete it. Your Gmail account keeps its own copy of your mail under Google's terms and privacy policy. If you configure backups, copies may be created according to your backup settings; those copies are under your control.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share Google user data with third parties. The app exchanges data with Google as needed to authenticate and provide email service. Optional AI processing stays on equipment you control and is not a third-party disclosure.

We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless you explicitly agree that we may review specific data for support, the review is necessary for security purposes, or disclosure is required by law.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information, such as an IP address, browser type, and requested URL, in accordance with GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself does not add any tracking.

Security

Qubes Mail stores OAuth tokens encrypted at rest and uses short-lived access tokens. Qubes OS isolates the application from other qubes. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect your device, your backups, and your Google Account with strong authentication.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Qubes Mail's access to your Google Account at any time through Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops token refreshes, but it does not delete mail already stored on your device.

You can delete local mail, the search index, and token files from your device. Uninstalling the app and removing its data does the same. Deleting local data does not delete mail stored in Gmail.

You can choose which Gmail folders to sync in the app's configuration. The default set is Inbox, Sent, Drafts, and Trash. To delete mail from Gmail, use Gmail or another Google tool.

This website

This site is a static informational website. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or JavaScript. It does not include forms or user accounts.

External links to Google, GitHub, and other resources are provided for reference. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

This app and site are not directed to children under 13. Qubes Mail is a personal email client and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be described on this page. Because this is a private application, we do not maintain a mailing list; check this page for updates.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app's privacy practices can be sent to the maintainer through GitHub. Please do not send email contents, passwords, OAuth tokens, or other sensitive information.

Google Limited Use disclosure

Qubes Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Without limiting the above, Qubes Mail:

  • does not use Google user data for serving advertisements;
  • does not sell Google user data;
  • does not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • does not allow humans to read Google user data unless you have affirmatively agreed to review specific data for support, the review is necessary for security purposes, or disclosure is required by law.

Qubes Mail is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google LLC. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC.
