Private application

Local-first email for Qubes OS.

Qubes Mail is a personal email application for Qubes OS. It connects directly to Gmail using OAuth 2.0, keeps a local copy of the folders you choose, and processes your mail locally in your own environment. There is no Qubes Mail account, cloud service, or Qubes Mail-operated server in the middle.

How it works Read the privacy policy

How it works

Syncs selected folders

Connects over IMAP using OAuth 2.0 and keeps local copies of Inbox, Sent, Drafts, and Trash.

Sends through Gmail

Composes and sends messages through Gmail's SMTP service only when you choose to send.

Works locally

Indexes and searches mail on your computer. Optional AI features use a local service on equipment you control; no Qubes Mail-operated server receives your messages.

Protects credentials

Stores OAuth tokens encrypted on your device and requests short-lived access tokens only when needed.

What it accesses

To provide these features, Qubes Mail asks Google for permission to access your Gmail account. The current design uses Google's full Gmail access scope (https://mail.google.com/) because IMAP and SMTP authentication with OAuth require it. Google describes that scope as permission to read, compose, send, and permanently delete email.

  • The email address associated with the Gmail account.
  • Messages and attachments in the folders you choose to sync.
  • Permission to send messages you compose.

Qubes Mail does not request Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google services.

Private by design

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or data brokers.
  • No Qubes Mail account and no Qubes Mail-operated cloud service.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, and access tokens are short-lived.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties. Data is exchanged with Google as needed to provide email service; optional AI processing stays on equipment you control.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics, or JavaScript.

Qubes Mail is a private application, not a public email service or commercial product. These pages document the application and its privacy practices for Google OAuth review.

Contact

Questions about Qubes Mail or its privacy practices can be sent to the maintainer through GitHub. Please do not send email contents, passwords, OAuth tokens, or other sensitive information.
